Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TeacherSwap

How does TeacherSwap work?
TeacherSwap connects teachers who want to exchange teaching stations across Tanzania. You create a profile with your current station details and preferences, and our Smart Matching algorithm finds compatible teachers. Once matched, you can communicate through our secure platform and coordinate the swap with guidance from our team.
Is TeacherSwap free to use?
Yes! Basic registration, profile creation, and matching are completely free. We also offer premium plans for teachers who want priority matching, advanced filters, unlimited messaging, and dedicated support to accelerate their swap process.
How are teachers verified on the platform?
Every teacher is verified using their TSC (Teachers Service Commission) number. Our system cross-references the TSC database to ensure every member is a certified teaching professional. This creates a safe, trustworthy community for all educators.
How long does it typically take to find a match?
Many teachers find matches within days of signing up. Our AI works around the clock to connect compatible teachers. Popular routes (like Dar es Salaam to Arusha) often match within 24 hours. Less common routes may take a few weeks as more teachers join the platform.
Can I arrange a temporary swap?
Absolutely! TeacherSwap supports both permanent and temporary swaps. You can specify your preference in your profile — whether you're looking for a 1-year exchange, 2-year exchange, or a permanent swap. Our filters let you find matches based on swap duration.
What regions of Tanzania are covered?
TeacherSwap covers all 26 regions of Tanzania mainland plus Zanzibar (Unguja and Pemba). Whether you're teaching in Dar es Salaam, Mbeya, Kigoma, Mwanza, or Lindi, our platform connects you with teachers from every corner of the country.
