TeacherSwap Terms & Privacy

By creating an account on TeacherSwap you agree to use the platform for the legitimate purpose of arranging teacher-to-teacher transfers and swaps in Tanzania.

We collect your name, email address and phone number to create your profile and connect you with other teachers. Your personal details are only visible to registered teachers you interact with, and are never sold to third parties.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide, including your TSC number and school details. Misrepresentation may result in account suspension.
